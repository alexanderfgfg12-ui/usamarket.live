Why Gold Remains a Safe Haven: The Case for Holding the Precious Metal in Uncertain Times

📰 Source: US Market Live

Amid geopolitical tensions and market volatility, gold has once again proven its worth as a safe-haven asset. But why do investors flock to the yellow metal during times of crisis?

The answer lies in its unique properties. Unlike fiat currencies, gold cannot be printed at will. It has intrinsic value and a history spanning thousands of years as a reliable store of wealth. When investors are fearful about inflation or currency debasement, gold acts as a hedge.

Central banks around the world continue to accumulate gold, diversifying their reserves away from the US dollar. This structural demand provides a strong floor for prices and reflects a broader shift in the global financial system. For individual investors, a small allocation to gold (typically 5-10% of a portfolio) can provide valuable diversification and peace of mind.

While gold does not generate income like dividends or interest, its lack of correlation with other assets makes it a valuable tool for risk management. In a world fraught with uncertainty, the timeless appeal of gold is likely to endure.
