Why Recessions are a Normal Part of the Economic Cycle

📰 Source: US Market Live

Recessions are a natural and inevitable part of the economic cycle. While they cause pain and uncertainty, understanding their underlying causes can help investors navigate them more effectively.

A recession is typically defined as a significant decline in economic activity lasting more than a few months. They are often triggered by a combination of factors, including rising interest rates, asset bubbles, or external shocks such as a pandemic or a major geopolitical event.

While central banks and governments can take measures to mitigate the severity of a recession, they cannot eliminate them entirely. In fact, trying to prevent every downturn can sometimes lead to more severe imbalances later on.

For investors, the key is to be prepared. Having a diversified portfolio, maintaining an emergency fund, and sticking to a long-term investment strategy are the best defenses against the uncertainty of a recession. Historically, markets have recovered from every downturn, rewarding patient investors.
