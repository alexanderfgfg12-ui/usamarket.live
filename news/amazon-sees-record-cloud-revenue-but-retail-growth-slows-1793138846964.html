Amazon Sees Record Cloud Revenue but Retail Growth Slows

📰 Source: US Market Live

Amazon reported record revenues from its cloud computing division, AWS, but growth in its core retail business slowed more than expected. The mixed results highlight the divergent performance of the company's different segments.

AWS continued to be the primary profit driver, benefiting from the surge in AI-related demand. Enterprise customers are increasingly migrating their workloads to the cloud and investing in advanced data analytics and machine learning tools.

However, the retail segment faced headwinds as consumers became more cautious with their spending. Higher prices and economic uncertainty have led to a decrease in discretionary purchases, impacting Amazon's e-commerce sales growth.

The company is also facing intense competition from rivals like Walmart, which has been investing heavily in its own e-commerce and fulfillment capabilities. Investors are watching to see if Amazon can successfully navigate these challenges and maintain its dominant position in both retail and cloud computing.
