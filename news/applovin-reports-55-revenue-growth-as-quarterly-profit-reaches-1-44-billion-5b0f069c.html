AppLovin Reports 55% Revenue Growth as Quarterly Profit Reaches $1.44 Billion

📰 BUSINESS | US Market Live
Share:

AppLovin reported record third-quarter 2026 results, with revenue rising 55% year over year to $2.185 billion and net income increasing 72% to $1.439 billion. The company’s results underline the scale of demand for performance-based advertising technology and software tools that help app developers acquire users and monetize digital products.

Adjusted EBITDA reached $1.842 billion, up 59% from the year-earlier quarter, according to the company’s release. The figures indicate that growth was accompanied by substantial operating earnings, although investors should distinguish the company’s adjusted measures from standard net income and cash-flow measures when assessing performance.

AppLovin operates in a competitive advertising ecosystem shaped by mobile privacy rules, platform policies, advertiser budgets and the effectiveness of automated targeting. Strong results can reflect better ad performance and increased customer spending, but the business remains exposed to changes in app-store rules, measurement technology and broader economic conditions.

The quarter provides a useful example of how software and advertising platforms can benefit from automation without owning the consumer apps or devices where ads appear. The next questions for investors include whether growth can be sustained, how customer concentration evolves and whether margins remain resilient as the company scales. A single strong quarter is evidence of momentum, not a guarantee of future results.
