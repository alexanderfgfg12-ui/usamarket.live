The Rise of Sustainable Investing: Balancing Profit with Purpose

📰 Source: US Market Live

Sustainable investing, also known as ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) investing, has gained significant traction in recent years. Investors are increasingly looking to align their portfolios with their values, while still achieving strong financial returns.

ESG investing considers a company's impact on the environment and society, as well as its governance practices. This approach can help identify companies that are better managed, more resilient, and better positioned for long-term success.

However, the sector has faced criticism, particularly around 'greenwashing' and the lack of standardized metrics. Some argue that ESG investing can lead to underperformance by excluding certain sectors, such as fossil fuels or tobacco.

Despite the debate, sustainable investing is likely to remain a major trend. As climate change and social issues become more pressing, investors are likely to continue to demand more transparency and accountability from companies.
