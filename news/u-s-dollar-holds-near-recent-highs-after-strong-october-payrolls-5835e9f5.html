U.S. Dollar Holds Near Recent Highs After Strong October Payrolls

📰 MARKETS | US Market Live
Share:

The U.S. dollar remained firm at the start of the week after October employment data strengthened expectations that the Federal Reserve may keep monetary policy restrictive. Reuters reporting published by Global Banking & Finance Review said the dollar index was around 102.20 early Monday, following a gain of roughly 0.7% on Friday.

The October report showed payrolls increased by 165,000 and unemployment edged down to 4.1%. Those figures were stronger than economists had expected and encouraged investors to reassess the likelihood of another interest-rate increase. The euro was recently quoted near $1.11485 in the same market report.

Currency markets can react quickly when expectations for interest rates change. A higher expected return on dollar assets may support the greenback, but upcoming inflation figures could alter that view. Traders will compare the next consumer-price report with employment data before drawing conclusions about the Federal Reserve’s path.
