Global Markets Rally as Tech Giants Beat Earnings Expectations

📰 Source: US Market Live

Global stock markets surged on Thursday, driven by a wave of better-than-expected earnings reports from major technology companies. Investors were particularly encouraged by strong revenue growth in cloud computing and artificial intelligence sectors, which have been the primary drivers of the recent bull market.

The positive sentiment was further bolstered by signs of resilience in the US labor market, with initial jobless claims remaining low. This indicates that the economy is holding up well despite elevated interest rates, easing fears of an imminent recession.

However, analysts remain cautious about the sustainability of this rally. Valuations in the tech sector are stretched, and any disappointment in future earnings could trigger a sharp correction. Additionally, geopolitical tensions continue to pose a risk to global supply chains and energy prices.

Looking ahead, market participants will be closely monitoring the Federal Reserve's upcoming policy meeting for any hints about the future path of interest rates. The central bank has signaled that it intends to keep rates higher for longer to combat inflation, which could weigh on equity markets in the near term.
