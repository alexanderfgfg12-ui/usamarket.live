Nasdaq Faces Pressure as Investors Reassess Big Tech's AI Spending Plans

📰 MARKETS | US Market Live
Share:

Technology shares came under pressure in late October as investors questioned whether the enormous cost of artificial-intelligence infrastructure will generate returns quickly enough. Reuters reported on October 30 that the Nasdaq 100 had fallen more than 5.8% during the month, putting more than $2 trillion in market value at risk.

The debate intensified after several large technology companies outlined higher spending plans for data centers, chips and related infrastructure. Investors are examining not only the pace of AI adoption but also how projects are financed, how quickly capacity is used and when new services can produce recurring revenue.

The implications extend beyond the largest technology companies. Data-center construction, networking equipment, power suppliers and semiconductor manufacturers are all connected to the investment cycle, although their exposure and ability to capture profits vary.

A falling index does not prove that AI demand is disappearing. It indicates that market participants are reassessing valuations, financing requirements and expected returns. Cash flow, cloud growth and capital-expenditure guidance will remain important measures in upcoming reports.
