Our rights-first publishing rule

USAMARKET CINEMA is a discovery directory, not a source of pirated copies. A film’s age, an archive upload, or a “free” label is not proof that copyright has expired. We will only add an individual title or embed when its public-domain status or licence and the source’s terms have been checked and recorded. If rights are unclear, the title stays out.

Rights can vary by country and by version, soundtrack, subtitles or restoration. This page is informational and is not legal advice.
