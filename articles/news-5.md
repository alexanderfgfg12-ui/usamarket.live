---
title: "Oil Prices Climb as Geopolitical Tensions Threaten Supply Routes"
category: "markets"
---

Crude oil prices rose sharply on Monday as escalating geopolitical tensions in key producing regions raised concerns about supply disruptions. Brent crude climbed above $85 per barrel, while West Texas Intermediate approached $82, reflecting growing risk premiums in the energy market.

Shipping routes in critical waterways have become increasingly risky, with insurers raising premiums and some tankers rerouting to avoid potential conflict zones. This has added significant costs to global energy logistics and threatens to tighten already constrained supplies.

For consumers, higher oil prices could translate into elevated gasoline and heating costs, potentially complicating the inflation outlook. Central banks have been monitoring energy prices closely, as sustained increases could delay anticipated rate cuts.

Energy stocks outperformed the broader market, with major oil companies and refiners seeing strong gains. However, demand-side concerns persist, particularly in Asia, where economic growth has been slower than expected. Traders will be watching inventory data and diplomatic developments closely for directional clues.
