# US Market Live — Organic app growth kit

## Distribution status to keep consistent

- Current public website download destination: `https://apkpure.com/usa-market/com.usamarket.mobile`
- The APKPure-linked package in the repository is the WebView app, application ID `com.usamarket.mobile`.
- A separate native Android project uses `com.usamarket.live`; do not advertise that package as publicly available until its release is signed, uploaded, and its listing is verified.
- The current public link is Android-only. Do not claim an iOS app is available. iPhone users can use the responsive website.

## 30-day organic growth plan

### Week 1 — conversion foundation
1. Publish and test `/download-app.html` on desktop and Android.
2. Confirm the APKPure listing opens, displays the correct app, and offers a current installable release.
3. Add the download page to Google Search Console and request indexing after deployment.
4. Track `app_download_click` in GA4; distinguish hero and bottom CTA clicks.
5. Capture genuine screenshots from the current public app for store and social use.

### Week 2 — searchable content
1. Publish one useful article explaining what US Market Live covers and linking to the app page.
2. Add a contextual app link to high-traffic market, technology, crypto and shopping pages.
3. Use natural, descriptive link text such as “Get the US Market Live Android app”; avoid keyword stuffing.
4. Review search queries and landing-page performance in Search Console and GA4.

### Week 3 — short-form video
Publish three short videos (15–25 seconds), each showing the real product:
- “U.S. market news in one place”: show the live site/app, market and news sections.
- “Explore beyond headlines”: show technology, crypto and video sections.
- “A quick tour of US Market Live”: show navigation and shopping discovery.
End each with: “Explore US Market Live — Android download at usamarket.live/download-app.html.”

### Week 4 — community distribution
- Share useful, non-spammy updates in relevant finance, technology and shopping communities only where self-promotion is allowed.
- Publish a short product walkthrough on YouTube and link to the landing page.
- Ask real users for honest feedback; never buy installs, reviews, ratings or incentivized engagement.
- Compare source visits, CTA clicks and confirmed installs where the store provides data.

## Measurement

Review weekly:
- Landing-page organic clicks and impressions in Search Console.
- Sessions on `/download-app.html` in GA4.
- `app_download_click` events by source.
- APKPure listing views and downloads, if those metrics are available to the publisher.
- Confirmed installs/active users only through a privacy-compliant analytics setup.

A click is not an install. Report store downloads and app installs separately, and do not promise a fixed download target before a baseline exists.

## Store listing compliance

Keep the title, description, screenshots and promotional text accurate to the currently published app. Google Play's official guidance limits the title to 30 characters, the short description to 80 characters and the full description to 4,000 characters; it also prohibits misleading claims and repetitive or unrelated keywords. See:
- https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13393723
- https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/4448378

Do not submit the native package to another store using the WebView package's listing assets or signing identity without first verifying the intended package, release key, privacy disclosures and store requirements.
